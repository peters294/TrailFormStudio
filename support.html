Getting started

Try the included demonstration route or choose Open GPX to import your own file. Trim the route, inspect the elevation profile, then open 3D model to adjust the terrain and route appearance. Use Export to save or share STL, 3MF or GPX files.

Terrain and offline use

The demonstration terrain works offline. For another area, download terrain from a supported source where coverage is available, or import HGT, GeoTIFF or ASC data. Downloads need an internet connection; cached data can be reused and removed in Settings.

Printing and files

Open exported STL or 3MF files in a compatible slicer to prepare the print. TrailForm Studio does not generate printer-specific G-code. If a file cannot be imported, check that it is a valid GPX file and is no larger than 20 MB. Large tracks are automatically simplified for processing.

Advertising privacy

Where available, use the advertising privacy option in Settings to revisit your choices. Ads may appear after saving an export, at most once per session.
